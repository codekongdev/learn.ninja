learn.ninja is a skill for your AI agent. Give the agent a topic, an article, notes, docs, a meeting transcript, or some code, and it writes **one short lesson** about the central idea, checks it, and publishes it. You get a link that anyone can open and play in the browser, on a phone or a laptop.

A lesson takes 5 to 10 minutes. It isn't a summary to read: it's a run of small screens called **bricks**, where you guess, practise, and get feedback on every answer. Try one:

## How it works

1. **Install the skill** in your agent with one command. See [Install the skill](/docs/install).
2. **Ask for a lesson**: "Make me a lesson from this article", or "Teach me how `map` differs from `forEach`".
3. **Share the link.** The agent replies with a link like `learn.ninja/l/how-compound-interest-works-k3x9a2mq`.

To change the lesson, just ask: "make it harder", "add a question about fees". The agent republishes to the same link.

## What it costs

Nothing. learn.ninja is free, and there's no account to create.

## Good to know

- Links last **24 hours** for now. Ask your agent to publish again for a fresh link.
- Lessons can be in any language, including right-to-left languages like Arabic.
- Lessons never run code. Visuals are plain HTML shown in a sandbox.
