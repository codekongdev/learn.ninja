A scenario brick sets up a situation and offers choices that are best, okay, or poor. After you choose, you see what happens next.

## When to use it

- Applying the idea to a realistic case, often as the lesson's final challenge.
- When there's a range of reasonable answers rather than one right fact.

## Fields

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `situation` | yes | The setup, in rich text |
| `options` | yes | Each with `text`, a `quality` (`best`, `okay`, or `poor`), and the `outcome` |

At least one option must be `best`.
