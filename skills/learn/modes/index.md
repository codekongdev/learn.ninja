# Modes

A lesson's mode sets the mix: how much of it teaches and how much it asks. The level sets how hard; the mode sets how many concept bricks there are and how many question bricks follow each. Every mode keeps the hook, the challenge, the takeaways, and at least one question after each concept, because recalling an answer teaches more than rereading.

## Pick the mode

Go down this list and stop at the first signal you find:

1. **The user's words.** "Just explain", "help me understand", "walk me through" → `explain`. "Give me practice", "drill me", "let me try" → `practice`. "Quiz me" or "test my knowledge" → `practice` for now. The table in `reference/planning.md` lists more of the words people use, like "practical", "hands-on", and "check my understanding".
2. **A child reader** (`reference/planning.md`) → `balanced`. Children keep an idea by using it often, and a lesson that's mostly reading loses them.
3. **The kind of knowledge.** Understanding how or why something works (a mechanism, a principle, a historical cause, a scientific idea) leans `explain`. Carrying out a method (a calculation, code, a grammar form, applying a design rule to a screen) leans `practice`. Many topics are both, like a design principle you then apply: if so, this step gives no answer; go on to the level.
4. **The level.** A beginner meeting a new idea learns more from explanation and worked examples; an intermediate or advanced learner with the basics learns more from solving problems. When steps 1–3 give no answer, beginners lean `explain` and the others lean `practice` only for a method.
5. **If still unclear, `balanced`.**

## Shape for each mode and size

Concept bricks × question bricks after each. Pick the size from `reference/planning.md`.

| Mode       | `short`                          | `standard`                     | `long`                      |
| ---------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| `explain`  | 2 concepts, 3 questions in total | 3 concepts × 1–2               | 4 concepts × 1–2            |
| `balanced` | 1 concept × 3–4                  | 2–3 concepts × 2–4 (max 8)     | 4 concepts × 2–3 (max 12)   |
| `practice` | 1 concept × 4–5                  | 1–2 concepts × 4–5 (max 10)    | 2 concepts × 5–6 (max 12)   |

The checker allows anything within 1–4 concept bricks, 1–6 question bricks after each, and 3–12 in total; the table is what each mode aims for.

| Mode       | For                                                   | Guide               |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| `explain`  | Understanding how or why something works               | `modes/explain.md`  |
| `balanced` | Most lessons: teach a little, practise it, repeat. **The default.** | `modes/balanced.md` |
| `practice` | Learning to do something: a method or a skill         | `modes/practice.md` |

Tell the user which mode you chose, and that you can remake the lesson in another.
